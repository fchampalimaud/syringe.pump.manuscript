{\large Significance Statement}
\newline
\textnormal{Liquid rewards are widely used in neuroscience behavior reinforcement. An open-source syringe pump designed for precise liquid delivery, while addressing the limitations of traditional gravity-based liquid delivery systems is presented. The new syringe pump system, built using off-the-shelf and 3D-printed parts, offers liquid delivery with a microliter precision and it can be controlled via software (e.g., Bonsai or Python) or through a low-level I/O interface. The system's accuracy is validated using computer vision and is shown to effectively manipulate rat behavior, with electrophysiological recordings revealing no electrical artifacts.}
